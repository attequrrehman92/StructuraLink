# StructuraLink — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 5 November 2026*

StructuraLink is a desktop application for structural engineers, made by Engr. Atteq Ur Rehman
(attequrrehman92@gmail.com). This policy explains what information the app keeps and why.

## What we collect

When you create an account or sign in, the following is stored:

- **Your account:** first, middle and last name, email address, country, phone number and job title.
- **Your password:** handled only by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.
- **If you use "Continue with Google":** your name and email address from your Google account. We don't ask for
  any other Google data.
- **Your license:** type, start and end date, license keys you activated, and when the app last checked it.
- **An anonymous code of your computer**, made from Windows' machine ID by a one-way hash. It's used to keep a
  license on one PC and to give the free trial once per PC.
- **When you send a report** with Help > Report an Issue or Request a Feature: what you write, attachments and
  pictures you add, and, if you tick them, a screenshot of the app and its last log lines.

## What we don't collect

- Your project files, models and calculations stay on your computer. They are never uploaded.
- No advertising, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

## Why we use it

- To create your account, check your license and keep it on one computer.
- To send you emails about your account (email verification, password reset) and license keys you buy.
- To answer your reports and requests.

## Where it is kept

- Accounts are kept with Google Firebase Authentication. Licenses are kept in Google Cloud Firestore, in the
  developer's Google project.
- Reports are emailed to the developer's Gmail.
- On your computer, the sign-in is kept encrypted with Windows' own data protection. It doesn't hold your
  password.

## How long

Your account and license are kept while you use StructuraLink. Email the developer to have your account and its
data deleted. It's done within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: attequrrehman92@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
